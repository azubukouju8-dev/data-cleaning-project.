# Decision log

Every cleaning decision, why it was made, and its trade-off. Exact row counts for each step are in `transformation_log.md` (generated by the notebook).

| # | Problem found | Decision | Why | Trade-off / risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Missing values written as `N/A`, `?`, `-`, `null`, `NaN`, blank | Convert all to real missing (NaN) | Otherwise they count as valid text values | A real value of "-" would be lost (none expected) |
| 2 | 16 exact duplicate order rows | Drop | Identical rows are export repeats | None |
| 3 | 7 orders share an `order_id` but differ slightly | Keep first occurrence | `order_id` must be unique; no timestamp to tell which is newer | The kept version may not be the most recent |
| 4 | Dates in 6 formats | Parse each with an explicit format | Automatic parsing guesses day/month inconsistently | **Assumption:** `04/03/2026` = 4 March (day first); `03-04-2026` = 3 April (month first). If the source used other conventions, some dates are swapped |
| 5 | Impossible date `2027-02-30` | Set to missing | Not a real date; any fix would be a guess | Those orders can't be used in time analysis |
| 6 | Quantity as text (`two`, `3pcs`, `1 `) | Convert to numbers | Meaning is clear | None |
| 7 | Quantity `0`, `-1`, `999` | Set to missing and add `quantity_flag` | Impossible or extreme outlier | Totals for these rows are missing |
| 8 | Prices like `$25.99` | Strip `$` and `,` | Needed for numeric analysis | None |
| 9 | Reported `total` doesn't equal quantity × price | Recompute; keep original in `total_reported`; flag `total_corrected` | Price and quantity are more trustworthy than a derived total | If a discount explains the difference, the recomputed total overstates revenue |
| 10 | Country: 23 spellings (`NG`, `nigeria`, `U.K.`, `England`…) | Map to 6 standard names | One label per country | `England` mapped to United Kingdom (loses detail) |
| 11 | Payment and status variants (`CC`, `COD`, `canceled`, `In transit`…) | Map to 4 categories each | Consistent grouping | `Refunded` merged into `Returned`; `PayPal` into `Mobile Wallet` |
| 12 | Emails without `@` | Set to missing | Can't be repaired reliably | Lose contact info for those rows |
| 13 | Names in mixed case / extra spaces | Strip and Title Case | Consistency | Names like "McDonald" would become "Mcdonald" |
| 14 | Orders with unknown product (`P099`) | Keep and flag `unknown_product` | Deleting would lose revenue data | Analysts must filter these when joining to products |
| 15 | Product `P002` appears twice with different prices | Keep the 79.50 version | Matches what orders were actually charged | — |
| 16 | Product with missing category (Notebook A5) | Fill with `Stationery` | Obvious from product name | Manual judgement |
| 17 | Product with missing price | Fill from median order unit price | Orders show the real selling price | Assumes price didn't change over time |
| 18 | `in_stock` as `yes`/`no`/`1`/`0`/`True` | Convert to boolean | One representation | — |
| 19 | Review lines: blank, header, `ERROR` lines | Skip | Not reviews | — |
| 20 | Review separators vary (`|`, `;`, tab, ` - `) | One regex splitter | Handles all formats | A comment containing " - " could split wrongly (none in data) |
| 21 | Ratings as `4/5`, `four`, `★★★★`, `4 stars` | Convert to integer 1–5 | Comparable ratings | `10/10` set to missing rather than rescaled (different scale) |
| 22 | Duplicate review lines | Drop | Export repeats | — |

## Limitations
- The dataset is **synthetic**, so real-world edge cases may be missing.
- Day/month order for ambiguous dates is an assumption (decision 4).
- Flagged rows are kept, so analysts must filter flags before computing revenue.
